Privacy Policy

Databillz keeps billing records local by default. Your records stay in your browser storage unless you later connect cloud sync.

Your Google account is used for sign-in and account separation, so each account can see its own records on the same device.

Databillz does not sell your data, publish your records, or send your transaction history to a server in the current local-storage version.

If Firebase, Google Drive, Gmail export, or another cloud feature is enabled later, the app may request extra permission before syncing or sending data.

You can remove locally stored records by using the delete controls inside the app or by clearing browser site data.
